Grow More Than Food

GreenRoots Community Garden is turning underused urban land into spaces that nourish both people and communities. What started in 2015 as two abandoned plots in Woodstock has grown into a network of gardens supporting over 300 members.

We don’t just grow vegetables—we create opportunities for people to connect, learn, and take part in something that has a direct, visible impact. By promoting sustainable living and fair access to fresh produce, GreenRoots is building a model of community that is practical, inclusive, and built to last. |

What We Do

Community Gardens

We transform abandoned urban spaces into productive community gardens that provide fresh produce and foster connections.

Educational Programs

Through workshops and school partnerships, we teach sustainable gardening practices and environmental stewardship.

Sustainable Living

We promote composting, water conservation, and other practices that support long-term environmental health.
